# Timeline — Tony Robbins

Synthesized from local-corpus extraction of 3 books — see `01-writings.md`
header for sourcing. This was the thinnest of the four locally-coverable
dimensions in every single book individually, but **cross-referencing the
three books together fills in real gaps** none of them provides alone — the
main value of this file is the merge, not any one source.

All dates below are reconstructed from relative markers ("X years ago," ages
at time of writing) anchored to each book's publication year
(*Unlimited Power* 1986, *Awaken the Giant Within* 1991, *Giant Steps*
mid-1990s) — none of the three books states an absolute birth date or year
directly.

---

## Consolidated chronology

- **Childhood, undated**: family couldn't afford a traditional Thanksgiving
  dinner one year; an anonymous charity box fed them. Stated as the origin
  of his adult Thanksgiving-basket philanthropy in *Awaken* Ch. 26.
  *Unlimited Power*'s **2003 reprint back matter only** (not the 1986 text
  itself) dates this to "age eleven" — flagged as secondhand/promotional
  sourcing, not part of the original narrating voice.
- **Childhood (~age 11-12)**: made to drink a six-pack of beer by his mother;
  vomited; never drank again (*Awaken* Ch. 3).
- **Hometown/high school: Glendora, California** (*Unlimited Power* ch. XIX,
  confirmed by his later return there to fund a teacher-training program).
  Not independently named in the other two books.
- **Age 13**: talked his way into a personal interview with Howard Cosell;
  began writing for a daily newspaper (*Unlimited Power* ch. IV).
- **High school**: a speech teacher's unsolicited encouragement redirected
  him toward communication as a career — he frames this (not any decision of
  his own) as the single most causally important turning point in his life
  (*Unlimited Power* ch. XXI). Known as the "Solutions Man" among peers, took
  a speed-reading class, read "close to 700 books" (mostly psychology),
  began attending seminars on his own dime (*Awaken* Ch. 5, 17).
- **Ages 17-19**: began deliberately pursuing a wide range of "reference
  experiences," especially via books, starting at 17; an 18-month spiritual
  "odyssey" attending ~12 different denominations/religions, triggered by a
  journalism assignment at Cal Poly San Luis Obispo (*Awaken* Ch. 17).
- **Age 18**: began annual Thanksgiving basket deliveries and joined the
  Chino, CA prison support system — both stated as starting "at the age of
  eighteen" (*Awaken* Ch. 26).
- **~Age 19-20 (~1979-80, per *Awaken*'s relative markers)**: low point —
  "a nineteen-year-old kid... alone and frustrated... floundering and fat,"
  working as a janitor in Glendale, CA, commuting in a 1960 Volkswagen
  (*Awaken* Ch. 1-2).
- **Just after 21st birthday**: first exposed to Gestalt therapy, Ericksonian
  hypnosis, and NLP; immediately became "an evangelist" for these techniques
  and launched a media/seminar career before completing formal NLP
  certification (*Awaken* Ch. 5). Separately, *Unlimited Power* places him
  "running my nutrition business" at ~age 21, with the Beverly Hills doctor
  encounter (ch. XIII) — plausibly the same early-career window, not
  independently dated closely enough in either book to confirm they're the
  same period.
- **~1983, "three years" before *Unlimited Power* (1986)**: living in
  near-poverty, variously described as a "400-sq-ft Venice, CA apartment"
  (*Awaken* Ch. 1) and "a dinky place in Marina del Rey, California"
  (*Unlimited Power* ch. XI). **Open question, not resolved by the
  corpus**: both books describe a formative goal-setting/visualization
  episode from this same general period producing the same outcome (meeting
  and marrying his future wife, acquiring the home/life he'd visualized),
  but with different specific settings and timelines — *Unlimited Power*
  frames it as a single Marina del Rey visualization exercise (6 months to
  meet her, 18 months to marry); *Awaken* frames it as a beach goal-writing
  session followed, six months later, by a more systematic rewrite on the
  back of a Russian map during a USSR trip with parapsychologists, with a
  *Life* magazine interview ~1.5 years after the beach session confirming
  the goals had materialized. These may be the same event told with
  different selective detail, or two related-but-distinct episodes in the
  same year — the corpus doesn't clarify which, and this file deliberately
  leaves it open rather than merging them into one clean story.
- **~1983-84 (via the above)**: met the woman he would marry (Becky);
  married her roughly 18-24 months later, i.e. ~1984-85.
- **January 1985, age 25** (per Ken Blanchard's foreword to *Unlimited
  Power* — **[secondhand, Blanchard's account]**): already described as "one
  of the leading thinkers... in the psychology of motivation and
  achievement" at their first meeting.
- **1986**: *Unlimited Power* published by Robbins Research Institute — his
  first book, written in under a month under deadline pressure by his own
  account (ch. IV). Age-25-in-Jan-1985 and the book's internal "age 25"-ish
  framing both point to a birth year of **~1959-60**.
- **~1986, "about five years ago" relative to *Awaken* (1991)**: discovered
  his business partner's embezzlement and ~$758,000 of company debt, after
  ~150-270 days/year on the road touring (*Awaken* Ch. 2, 15). *Giant Steps*
  independently references the same event as "early in my career," undated
  — consistent placement, no real conflict, just different precision.
- **~1986-87, "four-and-a-half years ago" relative to *Awaken***: took his
  children on a guided tour of a drug-ravaged South Bronx/Brooklyn tenement
  with the Salvation Army, intending to inoculate them against drugs
  (*Awaken* Ch. 3).
- **1984 (summer)**: the children's camp sexual-abuse-disclosure intervention
  — the most emotionally serious anecdote in the corpus (*Unlimited Power*
  ch. XXI).
- **"Eight years ago" relative to *Awaken* (1991), i.e. ~1983**: the central
  "I've had it" turning-point decision he credits with the whole subsequent
  transformation — losing 38 lbs and climbing from subsistence income to
  over $1M/year (*Awaken* Ch. 2). This overlaps chronologically with the
  Marina del Rey / beach-goal-setting period above; the corpus treats them
  as related but doesn't explicitly merge the narratives.
- **"Two years ago" relative to *Awaken* (i.e. ~1989)**: created POWERTALK!™
  monthly audio-interview magazine; began the *Personal Power* infomercial
  campaign (over 7 million tapes distributed in that window) (*Awaken* Ch. 4,
  8, back matter). Also purchased the Namale Plantation Resort in Fiji
  outright that same year, after ~20 prior visits over 6 years (*Awaken*
  Ch. 12, back matter).
- **1991, at time of *Awaken*'s writing**: states his age directly as
  "thirty-one" (Ch. 20) — independently corroborating the ~1959-60 birth
  year implied by the 1985/age-25 Blanchard data point in the earlier book.
  Back-cover bio: "a millionaire by the age of twenty-four," "founder of
  nine companies."
- **Mid-1990s**: *Giant Steps* published, explicitly framed in its own
  Introduction as distilling *Awaken the Giant Within* ("my #1 best-seller").
  States he has run seminars "for more than a decade" as of this writing
  (p.377) — consistent with the early-1980s seminar-career start established
  above.

## Family facts (consolidated, no conflicts across books)

- Wife: **Becky Robbins**.
- Children named consistently across *Awaken* and *Giant Steps*: **Jairek,
  Joshua/Josh, Jolie, Tyler**. *Unlimited Power* (earlier, pre-dating some of
  these children) names only **Joshua** directly in a parenting anecdote.
  Specific ages given at specific anecdotes: Josh was 6 when a friend died
  (*Giant Steps* p.207); Jairek was 4 during a described Thanksgiving
  delivery (*Giant Steps* p.323) — these are two separate, undated events,
  not a single dateable snapshot of both sons' ages at once.
- Maternal grandfather **Charles Shows**, a writer for Disney and later
  Hanna-Barbera, credited (per *Awaken* Ch. 8) with creating Yogi Bear and
  Huckleberry Hound.
- Mentors named consistently: **Jim Rohn** and **Richard Bandler** — both
  called "one of my teachers" in both *Unlimited Power* and *Giant Steps*.

## Internal inconsistency flagged, not resolved

*Unlimited Power*'s translation count is stated as "eleven languages" in one
part of *Awaken*'s text and "thirteen languages" in that same edition's
back-cover author bio — left as-is per the source extraction rather than
guessed at.

## Dimension gap note

This is the weakest-covered dimension even after merging all three books:
no birth date, no parents' names, nothing about life before age ~11, and the
corpus relies on relative time markers that require anchoring to each book's
publication year to even approximate absolute dates. A proper timeline
("06-timeline.md" in the full nuwa-skill methodology) would need either (a)
a biography/profile as a web-search supplement, or (b) more of his own later
material (post-1990s books, interviews) that this local corpus doesn't
include. This is the dimension most likely to benefit from the
"local-corpus-priority" model's supplementary web search, if that's the
direction chosen next.
