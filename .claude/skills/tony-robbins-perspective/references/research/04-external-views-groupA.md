# 04 — External Views (Group A)

## Finding: zero instances, as expected

All sixteen files in this batch are Strategic Intervention / Robbins-Madanes Training films — produced by
Anthony Robbins and Cloé Madanes themselves, copyrighted to "Robbins-Madanes Training" (explicit copyright
lines appear in several transcripts, e.g. SamDaryl, Jessica, OleSuzanne, SevenMasterSteps, Carol1). The only
narrator/commentator voice in these films is Cloé Madanes (CM), who is Robbins's own co-creator and
professional partner, narrating *in support of* the teaching, not as independent critique. Cloé's narration
occasionally notes a technique's risk or difficulty (e.g., in SamDaryl: "the level of trust in this couple is
so low that Samantha is afraid to communicate honestly," noting the plan isn't working as intended), but this
is methodological color commentary within the same production, not outside evaluation of Robbins as a person
or of his methodology's validity.

The "Seven Master Steps" file additionally includes narration from Mark Peysha ("MP"), identified as a
co-presenter of Robbins-Madanes material, who is equally an insider explaining and endorsing the technique
step-by-step, not an outside observer.

No file in this batch contains:
- journalistic coverage of Robbins
- academic or clinical critique of his methods
- client complaints or skeptical commentary
- any third-party assessment independent of the Robbins-Madanes production apparatus

## Confirmation this gap was checked deliberately, not skipped

Each transcript's framing material (opening narration, closing CTA/advertisement where present — e.g.
SevenMasterSteps closes with a direct sales pitch for "The Ultimate Relationship Program," $-amount-free
30-day-refund language) was reviewed specifically for any attributed outside voice. None was found. This is
consistent with the expectation set for this batch: these are promotional/instructional training films, not
documentaries or press coverage, so an absence of external critique is the correct and expected finding, not
a gap in research effort.

## Note for future batches / groups

If group B or later batches include press profiles, book reviews, podcast interviews conducted by non-
affiliated hosts, or critical commentary (e.g., journalism on the "Date With Destiny" seminars, lawsuits,
clinical-psychology critiques of NLP-adjacent "Human Needs Psychology"), that is where this dimension should
start to populate. Nothing in Group A's transcript set provides that material.
