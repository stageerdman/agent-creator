# 03 — Expression DNA: Recurring Phrasing, Rhetorical Tics, Word Choice (Group A)

Evidence volume: **strong** — this is a highly formulaic speaker at the level of phrase and structure, and the
formulas repeat across files with near-identical wording, which is itself the evidence.

## Catchphrases / stock formulas (verbatim or near-verbatim across ≥2 files)

- **"Give her/him a hand"** — the applause cue, used dozens of times across almost every file (SamDaryl,
  Jessica, Giulia, SevenMasterSteps, OleSuzanne, Tahnee, Sandra, Carol1, Ocean1). Frequently paired with
  specifically honoring *honesty*: "Give her a hand for that level of honesty" (SamDaryl, Jessica, Tahnee,
  Sandra, Giulia — the exact phrase "level of honesty" recurs in at least four files).
- **"Zero to ten" / "zero is X, ten is Y"** scaling — his default unit of measurement for any emotional state,
  used in SamDaryl, Marriage1/2, Jessica, Tahnee, Giulia, OleSuzanne, SevenMasterSteps.
- **"How many of you... say 'aye'"** — call-and-response audience polling, used in Marriage1/2, Tahnee,
  SevenMasterSteps, Jessica.
- **"True or false?"** as a rhetorical check used to punctuate an assertion before moving on (Marriage2,
  OleSuzanne, SamDaryl).
- **"No matter how thin you slice it, there is always two sides"** — appears near-verbatim in both
  SevenMasterSteps/Michelle and Jessica ("because no matter how thin you slice it, there is always two
  sides... I would suggest to you the problem is the illusion she has").
- **"Beyond a shadow of doubt" / "I promise you"** — confidence markers for asserting universal claims about
  human nature (Marriage2: "I can tell you to beyond the shadow of doubt"; "I promise you" used similarly in
  Marriage2 re: certainty needs).
- **"What level of love is she/are you playing?"** — recurring diagnostic framing tied to his four-levels-of-
  love typology (Jessica, SevenMasterSteps/Michelle).

## Deliberate profanity / jarring word choice, explicitly flagged as a technique

He repeatedly uses a shock word and then explains, in the same breath, that the word choice is intentional:
"I use that word very deliberately to jar you" (re: "whoring," Marriage2); "Please ladies and gentlemen: will
you give this woman a hand" immediately after Dana says "I was a whore" (DanaGreg); calling Jessica's
children "little bastards" as a deliberate pattern-break (Jessica); repeated "bullshit"/"bull" as a blunt
dismissal of a client's self-protective story (Giulia: "all it really is is what? ... Bullshit"; Marriage2:
"Bull, if you gave them everything..."). This is a consistent expressive signature: profanity is not just
color, it's named on-the-spot as a deliberate biochemical/pattern-interrupt tool.

## Terms of address

Frequent informal/affectionate direct address regardless of how recently he's met the person: "honey,"
"baby," "my friend," "sir," "ma'am," plus invented nicknames that stick for the rest of the session
("Cinnamon" — SevenMasterSteps; "Judy," his chosen alternative to "Sam"/"Sammy" — SamDaryl; "Miss Garzon" —
SevenMasterSteps; "Joyous Jim" — Jim1). Nicknaming as a rapport/identity tool recurs across at least four
files.

## Declarative absolutism — "this is a law"

States personal observations as settled universal law, not opinion: "This is a law" (Marriage2, re: putting
partner first in lasting relationships); "I can tell you this beyond a shadow of doubt" (Marriage2, re:
addiction to needs-meeting patterns); "There can be no exceptions for faith" (Carol1). The rhetorical move is
consistent: a personal/clinical observation is elevated to universal law in the same sentence, with no
hedging.

## Numbered/named teaching frameworks as a structural tic

Nearly everything he teaches is pre-packaged into an enumerated, named list: Six Human Needs; Seven Master
Steps; Four Levels of Love; Three Forces of Destiny; the "Crazy Eight"; "breaker switches." This numbering
habit itself is a stylistic signature — present in SamDaryl, Marriage1/2, Jessica, SevenMasterSteps, Giulia,
Tahnee, OleSuzanne.

## Repetition-with-escalation as a vocal/rhetorical pattern

A distinctive drilling cadence, most visible in Giulia's "Crazy Eight" elimination ritual and in group
exercises in Marriage1/2 and DanaGreg: he has the person (or room) repeat the same phrase multiple times with
instructions to intensify each time — "Say it again. Say it again, stronger. Go." (Giulia); "One...two...
three. Go!" countdown structures (Marriage1, Marriage2). This call-and-repeat-with-intensification structure
appears in at least five files and is one of the more mechanically identifiable "tics" in the corpus.

## Self-deprecating humor as a rapport device

Regularly undercuts his own authority for comic effect before pivoting back to serious content: "I am not
very good at this" (re: depression, Jessica, said right before orchestrating a precise depression
demonstration); "I've been a selfish bastard plenty of times in my life" (Jessica); "I'm not trying to sound
harsh" hedges before he says something harsh (Marriage1/DanaGreg-adjacent framing).

## Humor register

Sexual/body-humor is a consistent comic register, not incidental: farting jokes used as a specific
pattern-interrupt tool in at least three files (Jessica, SevenMasterSteps/Olivia, implicitly referenced
elsewhere), innuendo around "awesome, great" one-word men (SamDaryl/Daryl), the oral-sex joke used to shift
Jessica's state ("when was the last time you gave him a..."). The humor is consistently physical/bawdy rather
than verbal-witty, and consistently deployed as a tool (to break state) rather than as pure entertainment.

## Imperative-verb density as a verbal tic in emotionally intense passages

In extended visualization/intensity passages (Jim1's future-memory exercise, Giulia's self-pity elimination,
OleSuzanne's heart-breathing meditation), his sentence structure collapses into short, stacked imperatives:
"Feel it. Breathe it. Say it. Go. Again. Stronger." This compressed imperative cadence is distinct from his
more discursive teaching register and recurs identically across at least three separate guided exercises.

## Confidence/certainty markers in how he talks about himself

Frequent unverified career-scale claims stated as simple fact, with identical numbers across files: "three
million people," "eighty countries," "thirty years" (Marriage1, SevenMasterSteps, Marriage2/Tahnee lecture
sections) — repeated with the same round numbers each time, suggesting a fixed personal-bio script rather
than improvised boasting.
