# 06 — Timeline (Group B: Strategic Intervention transcripts)

## Finding: not applicable to this corpus, as anticipated

These are single-session (or two-session) coaching-film transcripts, not biographical or chronological material about Robbins himself. There is essentially no data here about Robbins's own life timeline — no dates of his own milestones, career turning points, or personal history beyond passing self-references used as coaching examples (e.g., his own ten-year high school reunion story in *Carol 2*, used purely as a teaching metaphor, not as biographical documentation).

## The one timeline-adjacent pattern actually present — noted for completeness, not counted as "Robbins timeline" evidence
A real structural pattern recurs across most of this group's films: each is constructed as **an original intervention + a follow-up interview months or years later**, used to demonstrate durability of change. This is a production/narrative device about *clients' outcomes*, not about Robbins's own biography, but it's worth recording since it's the closest thing to "timeline" data in the set:
- *Carol 2*: follow-up roughly **eight years** after the original (referenced, off-screen) intervention.
- *Lindsey*: follow-up **"a few years" (implied ~2+ years)** later, explicitly revisited on camera with both spouses.
- *Beverley*: follow-up **six years** later ("two hundred pounds" lost, "size 10 dress").
- *Shelia*: follow-up **five years** later.
- *Scott*: no long-term follow-up shown; resolves same-session via a live phone call.
- *Gary*: follow-up **two years** later.
- *Arontza & Alan*: two follow-ups — one **two and a half years** later, and a second session the **next day** at a different event.
- *Mike & Judy*: no dated follow-up shown within this transcript (single session, ends on the live breakthrough).
- *Karen & Warren*: two follow-ups shown — **six months** later and **one year** later, with a notable update that the couple had separated amicably but remained "best friends" and each found new relationships.

## Conclusion
No usable timeline data about Tony Robbins's own life exists in this transcript group. If a biographical timeline dimension is ever needed for this person, it will have to be built from a different source type (interviews about him, his own long-form memoir/book material, press profiles) — not from these session transcripts.
