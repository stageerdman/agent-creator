# How people feel at the four moments (for Merlin)

*Columbo, 2026-11-25. Builds on `updates/2026-11-25 FULL-UX - OPEN/journeys-columbo.md`.
Two-minute read. (A) = my assumption, not yet checked with real users.*

**The job:** people hire an agent to *"have the person who would know right
here, so I can ask them whenever I need."* Making it is rare and emotional;
talking to it is often and practical.

## 1. Home
- **Just before:** in the middle of work, a problem pops up, or they come back
  after a few days to check on an agent. Often in a hurry. (A: most visits are Talk.)
- **Trigger:** "I need Tony's take on this" or "is Marcus finished learning?"
- **In their words:** "Where's my guy? Is he ready? Let me just ask."
- **Feel:** before, a little pressure; during, recognition ("there they are");
  after, relief that one click got them in.
- **Magic, not pretty:** the agents feel *alive and waiting for me*, each one
  instantly recognisable, and the state (ready / still learning) is felt at a glance.
- **Breaks trust:** anything that stands between them and the agent they came for.
  Home that performs while they wait is an obstacle, not magic.

## 2. Creating an agent (the birth)
- **Just before:** they've read, watched or lived with this person's work for
  months or years. Usually evening, focused, a bit of time set aside. (A)
- **Trigger:** "I wish I could just ask them," finally turned into "let's try."
- **In their words:** "Will it really be *them*, or a cheap imitation? I know
  this person well; I'll know if it's fake."
- **Feel:** before, hope mixed with doubt; during, held breath; after, a jolt of
  "oh, that's them" (or a sharp letdown if it isn't).
- **Magic, not pretty:** the moment feels like *someone arrives*, not like a
  file is saved. It should feel earned and one-of-a-kind, a moment they'd
  tell a friend about. It happens once per agent, so it may take its time.
- **Breaks trust:** a grand birth for something that then sounds generic. The
  ceremony promises "them"; if the first words don't deliver, the magic feels like a lie.

## 3. Files flowing into the agent's DNA
- **Just before:** they've gathered books, transcripts, notes, often a big,
  messy pile they're proud of collecting. (A: dozens of files, some huge.)
- **Trigger:** "Here's everything I have. Learn it."
- **In their words:** "Did it actually take all of it? Which ones failed? How
  long will this take? Can I go do something else?"
- **Feel:** before, a mix of pride and worry; during, impatience and curiosity
  about what's sinking in; after, confidence that it *knows* the material.
- **Magic, not pretty:** they *see* their material becoming the agent's own,
  file by file, and can tell what went in, what's still going, and what
  didn't make it. Honest progress itself can be the spectacle.
- **Breaks trust (most fragile moment):** beautiful flow that hides whether a
  file really went in, a failed file that looks like a success, or progress
  that looks done when it isn't. Also any motion they must watch before they can walk away.

## 4. Talk
- **Just before:** stuck on something real: a client, a decision, a hard
  conversation. Many times a week, a few minutes each. (A)
- **Trigger:** "What would they say about this?"
- **In their words:** "Just answer me. And is that really from their work,
  or did it make that up?"
- **Feel:** before, a bit tense or stuck; during, focused, reading fast;
  after, lighter, with an answer they trust (or doubt that eats at the whole agent).
- **Magic, not pretty:** the feeling of being *in the room with that person*:
  their voice, their way of thinking, and the sense that the answer comes
  from their real words. Presence, not effects.
- **Breaks trust:** any delay before the first words; decoration on sources,
  quotes or errors; a confident answer outside what the agent learned. One
  made-up quote breaks the agent for this user.

## Across all four
- **Magic must never hide the truth.** State, sources, failures and progress
  stay plainly readable under any effect.
- **Big magic is for rare moments** (birth, learning). Frequent moments
  (Home, Talk) need quiet magic that never costs a second.
- **The person in the agent is real.** The magic should honour them, not
  turn them into a cartoon or a trick. (A: users feel protective of them.)
