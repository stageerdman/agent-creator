# Magic across the whole app (P1–P12)

*Merlin, Magic Director. 2026-11-25. For Jony (design), Eraser (cut review),
Columbo and the build team. Built on `journeys-columbo.md` and `update v1.md`
in this folder, and on the P2 world in
`updates/2026-11-24 AGENT-CREATOR - OPEN/magic-merlin.md` (shipped).*

Every moment below points to a journey step (`UJ# · step`). Moments marked
**MUST** are needed for the clickable .app to feel right. The rest are good
to have, ranked by impact.

## Summary

**The world stays the portrait sitting. It grows from one sitting into a
studio.** In P2 the user was the **witness**: the only one in the room who
knows what the subject really looks like. That truth gets stronger across
the whole app, not weaker:

- In **Make**, the witness brings **References** (the folder). The Sketcher
  **studies** them, and the likeness sharpens.
- In **Know**, the witness sees where the portrait is **Sharp, Rough or
  Blank**.
- In **Talk**, the portrait speaks. The witness is still the only judge.
  When a line is wrong, they say **"Not [Name]"**, and it goes back to the
  easel.

The human truth nobody uses: **a clone is only as trustworthy as its
witness is allowed to be.** Other clone tools treat the user as a customer
who uploads and waits. Here the user is the expert in the room. So every
moment answers one of three questions the witness keeps asking: *"Where did
that come from?"*, *"Is that really them?"* and *"What does it actually
know?"*

**The five must-haves for the .app:**
1. **Where from**: a source opens on the exact passage, in the user's own
   file names (UJ5 · 3).
2. **Not [Name]**: a wrong line goes back to the Sketch in one step, and the
   chat shows it was redrawn (UJ7 · 1–4).
3. **Studying**: the wait for a big folder. The user's own file names go
   by, first impressions arrive early, the Palette fills in, and there's a
   clear "Finished" (UJ2 · 4–6).
4. **What [Name] knows**: the map arrives Sharp → Rough → Blank, specific
   and traceable (UJ2 · 7, UJ2 · 10, UJ4 · 1).
5. **First words**: the first hello after Keep. The face fills in and the
   agent speaks first, in its own voice (UJ2 · 10 → UJ5 · 2).

All five are light: fades, marks, labels and one short reveal. None of them
adds a step to Talk. Daily Talk stays exactly as fast as plain chat.

## 1. The person and the true job

- **Make** (rare, deep): an evening, a folder collected over years, a bit of
  ceremony. The fear is "will it really read my stuff?" and, later, "is it
  really them?"
- **Talk** (daily, light): 7:40 am or 10 pm, stuck on something. The fear is
  "it's just ChatGPT in a costume." They want words in seconds.
- **The true job of the whole app:** *keep someone's way of thinking within
  reach, and make it honest about what it knows.* Magic in Make is about
  **recognition** ("that's them"). Magic in Talk is about **trust** ("I can
  see where that came from"). In Talk, magic must be almost invisible.

## 3. The world: the portrait studio

The labels stay plain. The world lives in microcopy, marks and motion.

| In the product | In the world | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| Uploaded material (P3) | **References** pinned by the easel | the witness's evidence |
| Inventory + skipped files | **Laying out** the references, some **set aside** | nothing is skipped silently |
| Knowledge vs persona split | two piles: **what [Name] knows** / **how [Name] sounds** | plain words, no settings |
| Learning (P3/P4/P5) | **Studying** | the Sketcher works through every reference |
| Early sketch + rough map | **First impressions** | something real, early, marked as early |
| Learning done | **Finished studying** | a clear "done" |
| Capability map (P12) | **What [Name] knows**: Sharp / Rough / Blank | where the likeness is clear, partly drawn, or empty |
| DNA / soul visual (P11) | **Palette**: what [Name] is mixed from | each reference and trait adds a colour |
| Agent chat (P8) | the portrait **speaks** | Talk |
| Source on an answer | **Where from** | the reference behind a line |
| Fix from chat | **Not [Name]** → back to the easel → **redrawn** | the witness corrects the likeness |
| Persona rating (P10) | **Likeness** | how close the *sketch* is, never a grade of the person |
| Material quality (P10) | **Easy / hard to study** | about a file's structure, never its worth |

**What success, failure, waiting and coming back mean:**
- **Success** in Make is recognition ("that's them"). In Talk it's an
  answer you can trace.
- **Failure** is a wrong line, and it's cheap: say "Not [Name]", and it's
  redrawn.
- **Waiting** is studying. You can leave the room. The work stays on the
  easel, even if you close the app.
- **Coming back** means the portrait is where you left it, and it remembers
  the last talk.
- **Nothing hardens.** Kept means *yours*, not *final* (P2 rule, still true).

### ★1 MUST · Where from

- **Journey and step:** UJ5 · 3 (check a source). Also UJ8 · 4 (quiz on the
  new book) and UJ2 · 8 (a voice card that quotes the material).
- **Before:** curious and on guard. "Did he really say that?"
- **After:** reassured, even impressed. "That's Day 3 of the course, word
  for word."
- **The magic:**
  - **Words:** under an answer that leans on the material, one quiet line:
    `Where from · Day 3 – Live Q&A.mp4 · 14:20  ·  Unlimited Power.pdf · p. 88`.
    It uses **the user's own file names** and a place they can find (page,
    timestamp, heading). Never chunk ids.
  - **Motion:** clicking opens the passage beside the chat. The exact words
    the answer used get underlined, drawn left to right in about 300 ms. The
    underline is the proof: "these words, right here."
  - **Honest absence:** an answer from the persona alone (opinion, voice)
    gets a muted line: `[Name]'s own view · no reference`. That's a pass,
    not a fail. It tells knowledge from personality.
  - **Sound:** none.
- **How long:** the line fades in about 150 ms *after* the answer finishes
  streaming. The underline takes about 300 ms on open.
- **Quiet when:** always quiet. It never delays the first words of the
  answer. No sources on every sentence, only where the answer leans on a
  reference (1–3 per answer at most). Reduced motion: the underline is
  there at once.
- **Mock data needs:**
  - For each mock answer: 1–3 sources with the user's file name, location
    (page, timestamp or heading) and a 3–6 sentence passage that
    **really contains** the claim, plus the exact span to underline.
  - At least one answer per main demo agent with no source, shown as "own
    view".
  - One answer that cites the newly added book (for UJ8 · 4).
- **Weight:** light. **Built by:** Jony (line, passage panel), frontend.

### ★2 MUST · Not [Name] (the fix loop)

- **Journey and step:** UJ7 · 1–4.
- **Before:** a jolt. "He'd never say that." Then mild frustration: "How do
  I tell it, and will it stick?"
- **After:** relief and authority. "I said it once, he was redrawn, and the
  next answer was him again."
- **The magic:**
  - **Words:** each agent message has one quiet action on hover or focus:
    **Not [Name]** (for example "Not Rex"). One word per action: it's the
    *only* way to change the agent from chat. A normal chat message ("be
    blunter") never changes the agent, so roleplay and tests stay safe.
  - **Carry the line:** clicking it opens the Sketcher beside the chat
    (the chat stays put). The wrong line arrives **pinned at the top**, as a
    torn-off strip of the conversation:
    ```
    ┃ Rex: "Great question! 😊 Let's explore that together."
    Sketcher: You said this isn't Rex. What would he say instead,
    or what's wrong with it?
    ```
  - **Fresh lines:** the Sketch then shows the change with P2's ★1 marks
    ("new"), plus a small tag `from your talk`. P2's "yours" rule holds: if
    the change touches a hand-written field, the Sketcher asks first.
  - **Back in the chat:** a thin divider appears where the fix happened:
    `── Rex was redrawn · persona updated ──`. Clicking it shows what
    changed. That divider is the "never silently" promise, made visible.
    The next answer comes from the redrawn Rex.
  - **Sound:** none.
- **How long:** the panel opens in about 200 ms. The divider fades in about
  200 ms. Everything else is P2 timing.
- **Quiet when:** the action is invisible until hover or keyboard focus, so
  it never clutters daily Talk. No pop-up ever suggests "this sounds wrong",
  only the witness decides. Reduced motion: the panel and divider appear at
  once.
- **Mock data needs:**
  - One planted wrong answer from the demo coach ("Great question! 😊…").
  - The Sketcher's reply quoting it, a Sketch patch with one changed persona
    line (`changed: ["persona"]`) and the "from your talk" tag.
  - The corrected next answer to the same question.
  - The real P2 chat can run this for real if wired; the mock only needs
    the carried-over line.
- **Weight:** light to medium (one action, one panel, one divider).
  **Built by:** Jony (action, pinned strip, divider), frontend, creator
  prompt owner (accept a pinned line as context).

### ★3 MUST · Studying (the wait, first impressions and "Finished")

- **Journey and step:** UJ2 · 4–6. Also UJ8 · 2 (a short 1-file run) and the
  library card of the demo clone that is still learning (UJ12 · 1).
- **Before:** anticipation, then worry. "312 files, will it choke? How long?
  Can I close it?"
- **After:** "It really read *my* files. I went for a walk and came back to
  him."
- **The magic:**
  - **Their own file names go by.** The main line names the file being
    studied right now, in the user's folder names:
    `Studying · Day 3 – Live Q&A.mp4 (listening) · 112 of 297`. Seeing their
    own files is the answer to "it never read my files". Names only, never
    file contents (privacy).
  - **Honest time:** `about 40 min left · you can close the app, it keeps
    going`. Stages in plain words: *reading*, *listening* (video, audio),
    *studying*. The estimate never jumps backwards without a reason.
  - **First impressions** (something to look at early): after a sample of
    about 20 files, two things arrive, both marked **"first impressions ·
    from 20 of 297"**:
    - a rough first Sketch (name, a persona line or two), in P2's faint
      style;
    - a rough first map: a few lines, all drawn in the "Rough" style. As
      Studying goes on, lines firm up from faint to clear, the darkroom
      feeling. A line only turns Sharp when enough references back it.
  - **The Palette fills in** (the DNA visual, early): beside the progress, a
    stand-in Palette gets one new colour each time a group of references is
    studied (books, seminars, Q&A). It's the one thing on the screen that
    is pleasant to watch, and it means something: "this is what he's
    being mixed from." The final look waits for Elon's research (P11). The
    behavior is set here.
  - **Finished studying:** a clear end state, never a bar that stops at
    100%:
    ```
    Finished studying · Rex
    297 references studied · 14 set aside · 1 couldn't be read
    [ See what Rex knows ]   [ Talk to Rex ]
    ```
    "Set aside" and "couldn't be read" open the list with a reason for each
    file. If the app was closed or in the background, a macOS notification
    says the same in one line: `Rex finished studying. 297 references.`
    The library card switches from "Studying" to "Ready".
  - **Sound:** only the system notification sound, which the user already
    controls in macOS. Nothing in the app itself.
- **How long:** First impressions arrive within the first minute of a mock
  run. The Palette adds a colour every few minutes of the run (instantly
  with the prototype's skip-time control). The "Finished" state stays until
  the user acts on it.
- **Quiet when:** the user can leave this screen at any time and keep
  working, including in the Sketcher. Studying then shrinks to one line on
  the agent's card and in the header. Nothing pops up until it's finished.
  Reduced motion: lines and colours appear without fading.
- **Mock data needs:**
  - A learning run with stages, a file list of ~300 named files (the UJ2 ·
    1 folder), a current-file pointer, counts and an estimate that counts
    down.
  - A skip-time control (prototype only) that jumps to First impressions,
    then to Finished.
  - First impressions: a rough sketch and 3–4 rough map lines, tagged
    early.
  - Palette stand-in: 4–6 colour groups, each tied to a reference group.
  - Finished summary: 297 studied, 14 set aside (with reasons: "Bonus
    folder, you said skip", "duplicate"), 1 failed video with its reason.
  - A "resumed after closing" state for the demo clone in progress.
- **Weight:** medium (the heaviest moment, still no new backend). **Built
  by:** Jony (Studying screen and card states, Finished card), frontend,
  a visual designer or Elon's research for the Palette look.

### ★4 MUST · What [Name] knows (the map appears)

- **Journey and step:** UJ2 · 7 (full map), UJ2 · 10 (short card at Keep),
  UJ3 · 2 (the "no references yet" card), UJ4 · 1 (the one place for "how
  good is it?").
- **Before:** "So what did it get from all that?" A little nervous.
- **After:** recognition ("yes, that's right") or a useful little shock
  ("Blank on money? Oh, I never added the money book").
- **The magic:**
  - **Order of arrival:** Sharp lines first, then Rough, then Blank, each
    group fading in about 80 ms after the one before. The best news lands
    first; the gaps come last, framed as a to-do, not a verdict.
  - **The first line is the most specific one**, in the person's own terms
    (a model name, a course name). That's the "it really got it" jolt.
    Generic lines ("motivation") are not allowed in the mock.
  - **Each line opens its references** ("why Sharp?": the 3–5 references
    behind it), using the same panel as Where from.
  - **Blank lines carry the next step:** `Blank · money and investing ·
    Add a reference for this`. A Blank is a place on the easel, not a
    failure.
  - **Behind the canvas:** the map always sits on a visibly different
    surface from the agent, with the line `Only you see this. Rex doesn't.`
    (P2 rule: the map never goes into the agent).
  - **At Keep** (UJ2 · 10), the Keep card shows three lines: the top Sharp,
    one Rough, one Blank. No sentence of praise.
  - **One-line agents** (UJ3 · 2): `Rex knows only what's in the Sketch.
    Add references to teach him more.` Honest, with a next step.
  - **Sound:** none.
- **How long:** the whole arrival takes under 0.5 s. After the first view
  per batch, the map just appears.
- **Quiet when:** the arrival plays once per learning batch. Every later
  visit shows it at once. It never blocks: the user can go to Talk from
  the first frame. Reduced motion: no stagger.
- **Mock data needs:**
  - A niche map for the demo coach: 3–4 Sharp, 2–3 Rough, 2 Blank lines,
    each in the coach's own terms, each with 1–5 linked references that
    exist in the mock file list.
  - The map must **agree** with the mock answers: a Sharp topic has a good
    sourced answer, a Blank topic has the honest "not trained" answer (see
    ★8).
  - The "no references yet" card for the stoic mentor.
  - The short Keep card.
  - A Sketcher answer about one gap (UJ2 · 7).
- **Weight:** light. **Built by:** Jony (map layout, Keep card), frontend.

### ★5 MUST · First words (the agent comes alive)

- **Journey and step:** UJ2 · 10 → UJ5 · 2 (the first Talk after Keep). Also
  UJ3 · 2 and UJ11 · 2 (the first open of an imported agent).
- **Before:** the hush after Keep. "Okay. Is it really him?"
- **After:** a small chill. "That's how he'd start."
- **The magic:**
  - **Motion:** in the chat header, the agent's face (photo or icon) is
    first drawn as a thin outline, the sketch, then fills in with colour,
    the portrait, in about 600 ms. It happens once in this agent's life.
  - **Words:** the agent speaks **first**, before the user types, in its
    own voice. Never "Hello! How can I help you today?". For the demo
    coach: `You came. Good. What have you been putting off?` It streams like
    any answer.
  - **A memory mark:** above that first line, a small, muted mark stays
    forever at the top of the first conversation: `First words · 25 Nov`.
    Months later, scrolling back, it's the agent's birthday.
  - **Sound:** none. (If the team ever adds one sound to the app, this is
    the only candidate: one soft note, off by default.)
- **How long:** about 600 ms for the face, then the first line streams
  (2–3 s for a short sentence). The input is ready from the first frame.
- **Quiet when:** once per agent, ever. Skippable: the user can type at
  once, and typing cancels the greeting (it shows in full, no streaming).
  After the first time, opening the agent shows the face filled in and an
  empty, ready input. Daily Talk never gets a greeting. Reduced motion: the
  face appears filled in.
- **Mock data needs:**
  - One scripted first line per demo agent, in voice (coach, stoic mentor,
    editor, imported agent).
  - An outline version of each face (or a CSS outline of the icon).
  - A "has spoken first" flag per agent, and the date for the mark.
- **Weight:** light. **Built by:** Jony (header, mark), frontend, persona
  owner (the first-line style; the real one comes from P5/P8 later).

### 6 · Laying out references (the inventory and the two piles)

- **Journey and step:** UJ2 · 1–3. Small scale in UJ3 · 3 and UJ8 · 1.
- **Before:** "Will it take video? I don't want the bonus junk in there."
- **After:** in control. "Yes, 3 books, 41 videos, 120 transcripts. Skip
  Bonus. Done."
- **The magic:**
  - **On drop:** the window says `Drop · your files stay on this Mac ·
    Claude reads them to study` (corrected in FULL-UX design, flag F1:
    Claude does read the text, so "it stays" alone was not true). The drop
    motion settles *down* onto the table, never up or away, so it never
    suggests a cloud.
  - **Laid out in seconds:** the folder appears as stacks by kind, in the
    user's own folder names, with counts and sizes. What can't be used sits
    in a separate stack: **Set aside · 14 · why**.
  - **Two piles:** a proposed split with plain labels: `What Rex knows`
    (books, transcripts) and `How Rex sounds` (live Q&A). The user corrects
    it in one sentence in the Sketcher chat ("persona from the live Q&A
    only, skip Bonus"). The stacks **slide** to their new pile (about
    250 ms) and the counts change. Under the Sketcher's reply, a P2-style
    change line: `Split: 18 recordings moved to How Rex sounds · Bonus set
    aside`.
  - **Sound:** none.
- **How long:** the layout appears as soon as the scan returns (mock: under
  1 s). Slides about 250 ms.
- **Quiet when:** it happens once per batch. A 1-file batch skips the piles
  and just says where the file goes. Reduced motion: stacks jump, no slide.
- **Mock data needs:** the ~300-file folder (UJ2 · 1) with kinds, counts,
  sizes and skip reasons; the split before and after the correction; the
  Sketcher reply restating the rules.
- **Weight:** light to medium. **Built by:** Jony, frontend.

### 7 · The map moves (Blank becomes Sharp)

- **Journey and step:** UJ8 · 3 (after adding the new book). Also UJ3 · 3.
- **Before:** "Did the new book make a difference?"
- **After:** the payoff. "There it is. Money went from Blank to Sharp."

  - The changed line **redraws** from faint to clear (about 400 ms) and

### 8 · An honest Blank (it says what it doesn't know)

- **Journey and step:** UJ5 · 4.
- **Before:** testing. "Let's see if it bluffs."
- **After:** more trust, not less. "It said it doesn't know. And the map
  said so too."
- **The magic:**
  - The agent says so in its own voice (the mock line is part of the
    persona, never a system message).
  - Under that answer, visible **only to the user**, a muted line: `Blank in

    map and Talk agree in front of the user's eyes.
  - The agent itself never mentions its map (watch-out).
- **How long:** appears with the answer, no motion.
- **Quiet when:** always quiet. Only on answers about Blank topics.
- **Mock data needs:** one "not trained for this" answer per main demo
  agent, on a topic that is Blank in its map.
- **Weight:** very light. **Built by:** frontend.

### 9 · The Palette (the DNA visual, full view)

- **Journey and step:** UJ9 · 2–3. (It first forms during ★3.)
- **Before:** curious. "What is he made of?"
- **After:** fascination, then ownership: "I can see the seminars in him.
  And I can tune him."
- **The magic:**
  - The Palette shows what the agent is mixed from: each colour area is a
    reference group or a trait, and hovering one names it (`Live Q&A · 18
    recordings · how Rex sounds`). Meaning first: every part is traceable.
  - Adjusting a trait (3–5 sliders or chips) shifts the mix live, in about
    300 ms, and the Sketch shows the change with a P2 "new" mark. The Palette
    is a view of the agent, never a second way to edit it in secret.
  - Label: `Palette · what Rex is mixed from`. Final look: open until P11's
    research.
  - **Sound:** none.
- **How long:** the first full open blooms in about 800 ms (colours
  spreading from the centre). Later opens appear at once.
- **Quiet when:** it's a place you visit, never on the Talk path. Reduced
  motion: no bloom, no live shift (the new state just appears).
- **Mock data needs:** 3–5 traits with values, 4–6 reference groups with
  colours, and the Sketch line each trait change produces. Marked
  "stand-in look".
- **Weight:** medium (a canvas or SVG view). **Built by:** visual designer
  after Elon's research, Jony (placement), frontend.

- **Mock data needs:** 8–15 talks for the demo coach with topic titles and

  next answer refers back.
- **Weight:** very light. **Built by:** Jony, frontend.

- **The magic:** each library card shows the face, the name and the top

## 5. No magic here

Places where magic would hurt trust or speed. Keep them plain.

- **Sources.** Never decorate a source, never show a source as a
  confidence badge, never put "sources" on an opinion. A source that
  doesn't contain the claim is the worst bug in the app, mock included.
- **Scores (Likeness and study scores).** No count-up animation, no red or
  green grades, no percentage alone, no celebration for a high number,
  no words like "captured". Always reasons first, number second. Study
  scores talk about a file's *structure* ("long transcript, no headings,
  hard to split"), never its worth. Self-cloners must never feel graded.
- **Private material.** Studying shows file *names*, never contents, in
  the progress line. Nothing animates *up* or *away* on drop. No previews
  of client calls or journals anywhere decorative.
- **Daily Talk.** No greeting after the first time, no typing theatrics,
  no "let me look in my references" narration, nothing that waits before
  the first words.
- **The agent and its map.** The agent never says "I'm Sharp at…". The
  map's words live only on the user's side of the canvas.
- **Auto-detect wrong notes.** No pop-up guessing "this sounds off". Only
  the witness says Not [Name].
- **Removing a reference.** Plain: `Removed. Rex no longer uses "Old
  seminar.mp4".` and the map after. No fade-away poetry.
- **Deleting an agent.** Plain, one confirmation that says what goes
  (talks, references). The agent never says goodbye. That would be a guilt
  trip.
- **Errors, CLI missing, a failed file.** Plain words and what to do.
- **Export and import.** Utility screens. The only "magic" is the honest
  "stays here" line.
- **Mock markers.** No moment may hide or style away whatever tells the
  team a value is mock.
- **Versions ("how he was last week").** Not decided (open question 3), so
  no time-travel moment yet.

## 7. All ideas I considered, and the cuts

**Kept above:** own-file-names stream · honest time line · first
impressions · lines firming faint → clear · Palette filling while studying ·
Finished card · macOS notification · Where from line · underline drawn on
the passage · "own view" line · Not [Name] · pinned wrong line · "from your
talk" tag · redrawn divider · Sharp → Rough → Blank arrival · Blank with a
next step · "only you see this" surface · face outline fills in · agent
speaks first · First words mark · "stays on this Mac" drop · stacks slide
between piles · was Blank mark · quiz suggestion · honest Blank link · trait
shifts the Palette · topic titles · last talk under the face · face
crossfade everywhere · Sharp lines on library cards · "stays here" export
line · "new here" mark.

**Cut, and why:**
- **A live portrait that slowly paints itself during Studying.** Beautiful
  once, heavy to build, and it implies the face *is* the knowledge. The
  Palette carries the same feeling and means something.
- **A percentage bar for learning.** Big folders make it crawl and lie. The
  file name, count and honest time say more.
- **Animated count-up on Likeness.** Turns a rating into a game show and a
  judgment.
- **Confetti or a chime on Finished studying.** Loud for a moment the user
  often isn't even watching. The notification is enough.
- **The agent greeting you every time you open it.** Charming twice, slow
  forever. First words happen once.
- **A "thinking" animation where the agent "flips through its
  references".** It's narration in disguise (P1 pitfall 11) and slows
  daily Talk.
- **Sources on every sentence.** Noise (D4).
- **Auto-flagging answers that "sound off".** Takes the witness's job
  away and will be wrong.
- **Letting "be blunter" in a normal chat change the agent.** Silent change
  (D7); roleplay and tests would break the agent.
- **A map drawn as a real terrain map with islands.** Needs explaining, and
  "Blank" islands read as empty, not as a to-do.
- **Agent says "I don't know that, add a book about it".** The agent would
  be speaking from the map. The user-only Blank line does it instead.
- **Goodbye message on delete.** Guilt trip.
- **Paper-tap sound on every Where from.** Sound in daily Talk wears thin by
  day two.
- **A torn-paper animation on the pinned wrong line.** The strip *look* is
  enough; motion adds time to a frustrated moment.
- **Library search across maps ("who knows about money?").** Useful, but a
  new feature, not a moment. Proposed for Jony and Eraser to weigh, not
  required.

## 8. Motion, sound and accessibility rules

- **Durations:** every motion is under 400 ms, except: the face fill-in
  (about 600 ms, once per agent), the Palette's first bloom (about 800 ms,
  once), and P2's fresh-line fade (about 4 s, never blocking).
- **Talk is never slowed.** No motion before or during the first words of
  an answer. Where from appears after the stream ends.
- **Once-only moments** (First words, the map arrival per batch, the
  Palette bloom, was Blank) are remembered per agent, so they never repeat.
  Typing or clicking skips them at once.
- **Reduced motion:** fades, slides, the underline, the fill-in and the
  bloom become instant. Marks and words stay, because they carry meaning.
- **Never colour only:** Sharp/Rough/Blank, "was Blank", "new", "yours",
  Studying states are words. The Palette's colours each have a name on
  hover and in a list for screen readers.
- **Screen readers:** the redrawn divider, the Finished card and the
  Studying line are announced politely (`aria-live="polite"`), not on every
  file change (announce every ~25 files or stage change only).
- **Sound:** none in the app. The only sound is macOS's own notification
  for Finished studying, which the user controls. One optional soft note
  for First words, off by default, if the team ever wants a sound.
- **Focus:** magic never moves focus. Not [Name] moves focus to the
  Sketcher input on purpose, because the user asked for it; Escape returns
  to the chat.
