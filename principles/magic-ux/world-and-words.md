  only judge of "is it really them?".
- Labels stay plain. The world lives only in microcopy, marks and motion.
- The single list of on-screen words is §6 of
  `updates/2026-11-25 FULL-UX - OPEN/magic-merlin.md`. Don't invent new
  names without adding them there.

  greeting after the agent's First words.
